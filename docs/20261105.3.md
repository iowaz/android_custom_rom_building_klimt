Prepared on 2026-11-05 for `lineage_klimt-cp2a-user`, signed with the existing private keys. The owner asked for a
per-commit review of https://gitlab.com/exonex39/risingos-klimt, a cherry-pick of whatever is worth it, a Codex

## The RisingOS review

`main` at `8b0d2ac` has 54 commits. 40 are misakazip commits already in our device tree's history (merge base
`630e71a`); each of the other 14 got its own reviewer, and Codex (`gpt-6.1-sol`, reasoning high) audited the
verdicts and the new commits. The reports, the disposition table and the audit are in
`diagnostics/risingos-review-20261105` (private).

One change was taken: misakazip `8b7b6d6`'s hunk that removes the 973 `vendor/etc/voicecommand/` entries
(MediaTek VoiceCommand training models, about 740 MiB) from `proprietary-files.txt` (device `a36c05f`). Nothing on
the build loads them; the VoW `audio_param` XMLs stay. The rest of `8b7b6d6` (moving shared MediaTek components to
hardware/mediatek) is not taken.

Skipped, with the reason:

- emptinens `ef2f478` (power and battery bundle): a 60 Hz default, an `init.project.rc` change that moves the torch
  and sipa permissions under `on charger` (breaks the `boot_completed` trigger), and PELT, uclamp, rcu, cpuidle,
  Wi-Fi DTIM and Perfetto changes with no failure behind them. Its
  `ro.surface_flinger.use_content_detection_for_refresh_rate=true` line was committed, then dropped before any push
  after the Codex audit: explicit `setFrameRate()` votes are honoured with it off, so the stated benefit was wrong,
  and it is unmeasured on this panel. It stays a possible A/B experiment.
- emptinens `8b0d2ac` (MTP USB HAL, camera recording, Monet): the USB rules target domains we don't run (our HALs
  are `mtk_hal_usb`), the camera props would switch off EIS/DSAC/MCNR with no bug behind them, and the Monet overlay
  is outranked by Evolution X's product overlay.
- emptinens `cb4d6dd` (RisingOS 9 bring-up, including a 1403-line vendor patch): RisingOS glue, things we have, or
  changes with no failure behind them on our build.
- emptinens `234387b` (status bar height): ours is 152 px from the stock overlay; theirs is about 106 px.
- emptinens `c246c95`, `609af72`, `67c4ba8`, `5818eeb`: empty merge, GitLab CI and template README.
- misakazip `b4a1dfd` (shared eSIM components): JP-only; it would turn eSIM off on the global unit and needs a
  hardware/mediatek fork.
- misakazip `fcf4148` (MIUI Gallery and Media Editor): about 370 MB of blobs, five conflicts, extra privileges and
  identity props. The owner can ask for it.
- misakazip `c97eec4` (shutter sound in every region): optional regional behaviour with no failure behind it.
- misakazip `af48e4c` (MIUI Camera) and `51f3898` (Dolby Atmos controls): already in the tree.
